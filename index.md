# Taylor Beebe

Taylor Beebe is an entry-level environmental planner. She seeks to utilize her knowledge of social and ecological systems to plan sustainable and equitable communities. She has expertise in environmental studies through coursework for her major and volunteerism at the Environmental Studies Center, Treasure Coast Wildlife Center, and University of Central Florida Arboretum. She has experience in planning through coursework for her minor and internship at the Palm Beach County Planning Division. She has a certification in ArcGIS and is proficient in Python and Microsoft Office Suite. During her education, she has maintained a 4.0 GPA in her major, minor, and certificate programs and created several class projects utilizing ArcGIS. These projects required her to create evidence-based urban and rural plans that addressed the unsustainable infrastructure of existing Florida communities. The work samples provided in her portfolio illustrate her passion for environmental planning.
